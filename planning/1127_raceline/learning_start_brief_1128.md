# Learning start brief, 2026-11-28

## Decision and scope

The target is high two-lap completion **and** low lap time across several course families, with particular attention to hairpin entry/exit, fast stable straights, and recovery from localization trouble. The 2025 course is a known example, not the final competition track. Qualifying and final course maps can be refined after disclosure (about 14 and 2 days before their events, respectively). On a changed physical course, keep the nominal mapped reference fixed and expose the change through physical occupancy/sensor input; never leak a reoptimized true map to the policy.

This is a **ready fallback launch command**, not a claim that all competition robustness work is finished.

## Reference decision before the next PPO update

Do not replace the training references with the old five joint-optimization candidates: all five lost original body clearance, and the 2025 example teacher crashed on geometry alone. The existing-margin 2025 pilot has much better closed-loop robustness on that one known map (frozen policy worst-mode 4/10 stored versus 10/10 joint), but is about 0.26 s slower in nominal two-lap policy runs and has no different-map qualification. The Practice existing-margin pilot is a gross solver failure: speed collapsed to 0.86 m/s mean and teacher success was 0/3. Keep original map references for the next PPO30 continuation. Let the already configured line guidance decay gradually so PPO can explore faster trajectories, while preserving completion gates. Continue minimum-time reference optimization as a separately audited track-specific improvement; no candidate is promoted merely because its model lap time or sampled constraints look good.

The optimizer now reports a gross source-relative planned-time sanity check (default rejection above 2x) in addition to dense force, steering, body-clearance, and static raster checks. `audit_reference_clearance_1128.py` measures each candidate against its own source grid; this handles narrow-map exceptions without a global margin. A candidate then needs matched nominal/worst two-lap policy and teacher validation, independent seeds and geometries, and no material lap-time regression before promotion. Static and model checks alone are insufficient.

## Remaining preparation for the full robustness target

The existing shared-scan benchmark showed that close same-direction traffic can cause opponent collision while localization is still correct, and that a conservative sensor-only braking envelope trades pace for safety. Training does **not yet** include a reactive second car, relative gap/closing-speed observations, or actual scan-localizer loss/reacquisition. Add and validate those before claiming robust multi-car performance; test different starting gaps and physical wall shifts with a fixed nominal map. Keep the clean-track pace cohort so a safety change does not silently slow all laps. The existing map-drift generator and actual scan-localizer benchmark are preparation evidence, not training integration.

The paired three-seed close-traffic audit (`traffic_seed_audit_1128.md`) makes the tradeoff concrete: raw policy 0/3 complete against a nearby scripted car; sensor-only braking envelope 3/3, but +0.758 s mean on clean two-lap runs. It is a diagnostic on one known map, not a traffic-ready policy. The PPO30 resume assets are present: 736 regular, 184 open, 90 short, 101 hall, and 2 real training map files, with zero missing; generated regular and open sets passed their freshness checks. This validates a stored-reference resume path, not the full traffic/localization target.

The fixed-map, clean Practice warp audit completed 3/3 at 0.05 m and 3/3 at 0.10 m with nearly unchanged times. That narrow result keeps barrier drift in the evaluation plan but does not justify relaxing the traffic or localization gates, especially since the existing combined warp-plus-close-traffic run failed raw.

The reference optimizer also still needs an existing-margin, solver-quality, closed-loop promotion decision on regular, open, and narrow families. These are not blockers to resuming PPO30 on **stored references**, but they are blockers to a full optimized-reference or competition-ready claim. Future mapped competition courses require map-specific line/speed refinement under the same hardware envelope and closed-loop gates before on-car use.
